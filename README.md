## 🎯 Project Aim
The objective of this analysis was to leverage **Power BI** and **SQL Server** to gain actionable insights from pizza sales data.  
Key goals:  
- Identify sales trends and peak periods  
- Determine customer preferences (pizza size, category, and type)  
- Highlight low-performing products and opportunities  
- Support strategic business and promotional decisions  

---

## 🔍 Workflow & Process

### 1️⃣ Data Extraction
- Connected to **SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS)** to extract transactional pizza sales data.  
- Queried tables for **order details, product category, pizza size, sales amount, and timestamps**.  

### 2️⃣ Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Standardized column names and data types for consistency.  
- Checked for missing or inconsistent values (e.g., null pizza size or category).  
- Aggregated sales by category, size, day, and month for analysis.

### 3️⃣ Exploratory Analysis
- Calculated **total revenue**, **average order value**, and **sales volume by category and size**.  
- Evaluated **temporal trends** (day of week, month, and hour).  
- Identified top-selling and low-performing pizzas using SQL queries.

### 4️⃣ Visualization & Dashboarding (Power BI)
- Built interactive **Power BI dashboards** with KPIs and slicers for **Category, Pizza Type, Size, and Time Period**.  
- Created visuals for **Revenue by Pizza Type**, **Sales by Day/Month**, and **Top-Selling Sizes**.  
- Designed executive-level metrics for quick decision-making and reporting.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Results

### ✅ Revenue & Sales Performance
- **Total Revenue:** $817.86K  
- **Top Revenue Pizza:** Thai Chicken Pizza  
- **Lowest Revenue Pizza:** Brie Carre Pizza  
- **Revenue by Category:** Classic > Supreme > Veggie  

### ✅ Top-Selling Pizza Size & Category
- **Most Preferred Size:** Large (45% of total sales)  
- **Highest Revenue Pizza:** Classic Deluxe Pizza  

### ✅ Order Trends & Customer Behavior
- **Busiest Days:** Fridays and Saturdays  
- **Peak Months:** January, May, July, March  
- **Average Order Size:** 2–3 pizzas per customer  
- **Peak Hours:** Evening sales highest  

### ✅ Low-Performing Products
- **Lowest Sales:** Brie Carre Pizza  
- **Less Popular Options:** Small-sized and gluten-free pizzas (<10% total sales)  

---

## 💡 Recommendations

1️⃣ **Boost High-Performing Pizzas & Sizes**  
- Focus promotions on **Thai Chicken** and **Classic Deluxe Pizzas**.  
- Increase stock of **large-sized pizzas** to meet demand.  

2️⃣ **Target Peak Sales Periods**  
- Launch **weekend offers** and **Friday/Saturday discounts**.  
- Run **seasonal campaigns** in January, May, and July.  

3️⃣ **Revive Low-Performing Products**  
- Revamp the **Brie Carre Pizza recipe** or introduce limited-time deals.  
- Use **creative marketing** for gluten-free and small-sized pizzas to attract health-conscious customers.
